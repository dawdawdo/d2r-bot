Quick answer · lowest-requirement Call to Arms base: a War Staff has no Str/Dex requirement, but Larzuk gives it 6 sockets, so it needs a natural 5-socket drop. The lowest you can make with Larzuk is a Long Bow (22 Str / 19 Dex, item level 41+ gets exactly 5). Lowest one-hander: Double Axe (43 Str, 5 from Larzuk). Your plan’s Flail (41 Str / 35 Dex) works too. Call to Arms itself needs level 57.
